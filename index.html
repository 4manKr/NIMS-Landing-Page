About the College

National Institute of Medical Sciences & Research, Jaipur

Established in 2004 and affiliated to NIMS University, Jaipur, NIMS Medical College & Hospital offers the MBBS programme with an annual intake of 250 students. Admission is strictly through NEET merit and counselling.

The campus on the Jaipur–Delhi Highway houses modern lecture halls, laboratories and a large library, with an attached 1,130-bed teaching hospital where students get hands-on exposure in diagnostics, patient care and emergency medicine.
